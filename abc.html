ABC Trek

The Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Trek is one of Nepal’s most popular and scenic treks, leading hikers into the Annapurna Sanctuary at an altitude of 4,130 meters (13,550 feet). This moderate-grade, out-and-back route is renowned for its 360-degree panoramic views of towering peaks including Annapurna I, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), Hiunchuli, and Annapurna South.

The trail traverses diverse landscapes, ranging from subtropical rhododendron and bamboo forests to steep stone staircases and high alpine meadows. Trekkers pass through traditional Gurung and Magar villages, such as Ghandruk and Chhomrong, experiencing local culture and staying in family-run teahouses along the way.

Typical itineraries range from 5 to 12 days, covering distances of approximately 65 to 90 kilometers round-trip. The trek is considered beginner-friendly regarding technical difficulty, requiring no mountaineering skills, but demands good physical fitness for daily walks of 5 to 8 hours and proper acclimatization to high altitude.
