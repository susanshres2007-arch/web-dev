Michael is Great

Archangel Michael is a principal celestial figure in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam, revered as the supreme commander of the heavenly hosts and the warrior of God. His name derives from the Hebrew Mīkhā'ēl, meaning "Who is like God?" (Quis ut Deus in Latin), a rhetorical challenge to the pride of Satan. Unlike human saints, Michael is a pure spirit and an archangel, though he is honored with the title "Saint" in Christian traditions to signify his holiness.
